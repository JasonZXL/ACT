Combat Focus Mechanic Notes

Document Purpose
This document records the currently agreed design for the player's Focus resource in the combat prototype.
It is intended as a stable reference before continuing into stagger design and combat scripting.

========================================
1. Core Role Of Focus
========================================

Focus is the player's main combat resource.
It exists to give the player:
- subjective initiative
- action priority
- a reason to choose between light attacks, heavy attacks, defense, and finishers

Without Focus, all offensive and defensive tools would compete on equal footing and the combat loop would lack hierarchy.

Focus should therefore act as:
- a tempo resource
- a reward for correct offensive and defensive play
- a gate for higher-value combat actions

========================================
2. Focus Capacity
========================================

Focus is divided into three segments.

Rules:
- Maximum Focus = 3 segments
- Each segment is a meaningful spending unit

Basic spending idea:
- 1 segment = one standard heavy attack

========================================
3. Focus Generation
========================================

Focus is mainly earned through active and successful combat actions.
It should not be a passive recovery resource.

Current proposed generation values:

- Light attack hit: +0.25
- Run attack hit: +0.5
- Heavy attack hit: +0.1
- Perfect dodge: +0.5
- Perfect dodge followed by a successful punish within a short time: additional +0.5
- Successful parry: +1
- Successful parry counter hit: additional +0.5
- Execution: +1
- Effective follow-up on a staggered enemy: +0.25

Design intention:
- Light attacks are the main stable Focus builder
- Run attacks accelerate tempo
- Perfect dodge and parry are high-skill accelerators
- Heavy attack can refund a small amount but must never sustain itself
- Execution is a tempo reward

========================================
4. Dodge And Focus
========================================

Normal dodge should not grant Focus.

Current rule:
- Normal dodge: +0
- Perfect dodge: +0.5
- If the player successfully punishes shortly after a perfect dodge: additional +0.5

Design reason:
- Prevent players from farming Focus by repeatedly dodging without engaging
- Reward defense only when it leads back into offense

========================================
5. Parry And Focus
========================================

Parry should be more rewarding than dodge because it is riskier and more proactive.

Current rule:
- Regular guard: +0
- Successful timed parry: +1
- Successful parry counter hit afterward: additional +0.5

Design reason:
- Dodge is the safer answer
- Parry is the higher-reward answer

========================================
6. Passive Recovery
========================================

Focus should not slowly regenerate during battle.

Current rule:
- No passive auto-recovery during combat

Design reason:
- Focus must feel earned through combat actions
- Prevent players from disengaging and waiting for resources to refill

========================================
7. Focus At Maximum
========================================

Focus reaching full should not feel wasteful.
A full gauge should increase pressure to spend it efficiently.

Current design direction:
- Full Focus should enable stronger spending options
- Higher-tier attacks should become available or more efficient when Focus is full

Recommended tier structure:
- 1 segment: standard heavy attack
- 2 segments: stronger heavy / higher stagger / stronger break attack
- 3 segments: special finisher-level attack or wave attack

Design reason:
- Full Focus creates meaningful decision pressure
- Spending higher amounts should feel like stronger tempo conversion

========================================
8. Focus Decay Out Of Combat
========================================

Focus must not be permanently banked before elite fights.
However, it also should not disappear instantly in a way that feels bad.

Current rule:
- Focus does not persist indefinitely between combat encounters
- Focus starts decaying 2 seconds after leaving combat
- Focus decays at 1 segment per second
- Minimum retained Focus after full decay: 0

Design reason:
- Prevent pre-farming Focus on weak enemies before stronger encounters
- Keep Focus as an in-combat tempo resource, not a stockpiled reserve

Important consequence:
- Focus is a battle rhythm resource, not a preparation resource

========================================
9. Heavy Attack Relationship To Focus
========================================

Heavy attacks are resource spenders, not primary generators.
However, they may refund a small amount on hit.

Current rule:
- Standard heavy attack costs 1 Focus
- Heavy attack hit may refund a small amount such as +0.1

Design reason:
- Heavy attacks should not feel like pure loss
- Heavy attacks must never become self-sustaining loops

========================================
10. Wave Attack Role
========================================

Wave attack should not be treated as a basic light attack.
It should also not be totally disconnected from the Focus system.

Current agreed direction:
- Wave attack belongs to a higher-tier offensive branch
- It is closer to a special finisher or advanced heavy attack than to a normal light attack

Recommended role:
- Special finisher attack
- High-value resource spender
- Safe ender / ranged pressure ender / stagger conversion tool

Recommended cost direction:
- Prefer 3 Focus for the full version
- A reduced or downgraded version may be used if Focus is insufficient, but the preferred design is that the true wave attack is a Focus-based finisher

Design reason:
- Prevent wave attack from becoming a low-cost spam tool
- Preserve the value of Focus as a priority-setting resource

========================================
11. Player Decision Hierarchy Created By Focus
========================================

Focus should create a natural action priority structure.

Current intended hierarchy:

- Light attack:
  Build Focus and chip enemy stagger

- Run attack:
  Re-enter tempo and build Focus faster

- Heavy attack:
  Spend Focus for higher stagger, interruption, and pressure

- Dodge:
  Safe defense, with perfect dodge enabling better Focus gain

- Parry:
  High-risk, high-reward defense and strong Focus gain

- Execution:
  Reward after successful pressure and stagger conversion

- Wave / finisher attack:
  High-value spend option, likely tied to full or near-full Focus

========================================
12. Summary
========================================

The Focus system is currently defined as follows:

- Three total segments
- Built mainly through successful offense and high-quality defense
- No passive combat regeneration
- Decays after leaving combat
- Heavy attacks consume it
- Wave attack should be a higher-tier finisher that meaningfully interacts with Focus
- The purpose of Focus is to create player initiative, action priority, and combat planning

This Focus design is now considered the current working baseline for the combat prototype.
